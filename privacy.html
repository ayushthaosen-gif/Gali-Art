Privacy & Terms

What we collect

When you send an order request we get your name, your email address, what you picked and any notes you add. We use that to handle your order and to reply to you.

If you personalise a poster, the places, text and date you enter are used only to make and proof your poster.

Who sees it

Your request reaches us by email, or through our form provider. We don't sell your data and the site has no advertising trackers. The page loads the Jost, DM Mono and Noto Sans Devanagari fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser does contact Google to get them.

Finding a place on the map

If you press "Find on map", the place you typed is sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim search service to get its position. Nothing is sent while you type, and we don't send your name or email. A map link you paste is read in your browser without being sent anywhere.

Your choices

Email us if you want a copy of your data, or want it deleted.

Orders & terms (draft)

Posters are made to order. Before you pay, we email you the price, the delivery time and how to pay. Sending a request here isn't a purchase until we confirm it.

Map data

Maps are based on data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence.
