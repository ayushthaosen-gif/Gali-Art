Privacy & Terms

What we collect

When you submit the order form we receive your name, email address, poster choices and any notes you add. We use them only to process and fulfil your order and to reply to you.

Who sees it

Your order request is sent to us by email (or through our form provider). We do not sell your data. This site does not use advertising trackers. Poster previews load the Jost, DM Mono and Noto Sans Devanagari fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google to fetch them.

Your choices

Email us to ask for a copy of your data or to have it deleted.

Orders & terms (draft)

Posters are made to order. We confirm the price, delivery time and how to pay by email before you pay. A request on this site is not a purchase until we confirm it.
