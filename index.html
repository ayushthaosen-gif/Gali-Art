    FAQ

    Questions

    Is this a real map?

    Yes. Posters are drawn from real OpenStreetMap street data. Delhi also has a 1995 edition, which shows today's streets within the city's 1995 built-up area (an approximation, not a historical survey).

    Why are there no street names?

    The street shapes are the design. A map without labels lets the city speak for itself, with just its name, coordinates and year beneath.

    Can I get a different city?

    Pick "Other" in the order form and tell us which one. We are adding more cities.

    Which sizes are available?

    A4, A3 and 18 × 24 inch, with or without a frame. Prices update live in the order form.

    Can I get it framed?

    Yes. Choose black wood, natural oak or white wood (a 20 mm profile with acrylic glazing). Oak and white can add an off-white mat. The frame price is added to the total before you send your request.

    How do I pay?

    Send your request and we'll reply by email with the next steps, including how to pay.
