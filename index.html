FAQ

Questions

Is this a real map?

Yes, it comes from OpenStreetMap data. Delhi also has a 1995 version, but that isn't an old map. It shows today's streets, cut down to the area that was built up in 1995.

Why are there no street names?

Labels get in the way. Without them you see the shape of the streets, with just the city name, coordinates and year underneath.

Can I get a different city?

Choose "Other city" in the order form and tell us which one. We're adding more.

Which sizes are available?

A4, A3 and 18 × 24 inch, framed or not. The order form shows the price as you choose.

Can I get it framed?

Yes. You can pick black wood, natural oak or white wood. They are all a 20 mm frame with acrylic glazing, and oak and white can have an off-white mat too. The total updates as you choose.

How do I pay?

Send the request first. We'll email you back with what happens next, including how to pay.
